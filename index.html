Questions

Frequently asked

Pulled from real questions our 300+ Telegram members have actually asked us.

Is this only for 11th graders?

No. FLEX is open to students in the eligible age and grade range set by the program each year, and our UWC and YYGS tracks are open more broadly. If you're not sure whether you qualify, message us on Telegram and we'll check your specific grade and birth year against the current program rules.

What if my English level is low?

Plenty of successful applicants start with modest English. Our FLEX track includes dedicated ELTiS and reading prep built for exactly this, and mentors adjust pacing to where you're actually starting from — not where the syllabus assumes you are.

What happens if I don't get in?

We'll be honest: these are competitive, low-acceptance programs, and we can't guarantee an outcome. What we can guarantee is a stronger application than you'd have built alone, and mentors who'll help you decide whether to reapply the following year.

How is the course structured — live sessions or recorded?

A mix. Core teaching happens live so you can ask questions in the moment, and sessions are recorded for anyone who misses one. Essay and interview feedback is always one-on-one.

Is there a payment plan?

Yes — message us on Telegram and we'll set up a plan that fits your situation. We built this course to be accessible, and we mean that in how you can pay for it too.

Who is actually teaching the course?

Our FLEX mentors, Ismail Mamadaliyev and Azizbek Torabayev, are FLEX alumni who went through the program themselves. Our UWC track is mentored by a student currently preparing for their own UWC departure. We don't outsource teaching to tutors who haven't been through these programs.
